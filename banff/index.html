Check before every outing

Weather, smoke, closures, roads, and trail conditions decide which listed option is practical. They do not change the fixed flights, stays, or rental car.

Parks Canada fire alerts ↗ Alberta wildfire status ↗ Emergency alerts ↗ Alberta 511 roads ↗
  1. Check the next location’s Environment Canada forecast and AQHI.
  2. Check Parks Canada bulletins and trail conditions, Alberta 511, and shuttle status.
  3. Choose one primary outdoor objective and one indoor or low-exertion fallback.
  • Emergency alert, evacuation order, or closure: stop the affected plan and follow instructions. Never drive toward it or route around it.
  • AQHI 7+, poor visibility, or smoke symptoms: skip hikes, gondola viewpoints, and long scenic stops. Favor nearby indoor/low-exertion time, meals, driving, or rest.
  • Fire bans: check the specific park or accommodation rule before using any flame, firepit, charcoal grill, or stove.
  • Wildlife tour: the operator’s weather/smoke decision controls the Aug 2 tour. Keep their message and contact details available.
