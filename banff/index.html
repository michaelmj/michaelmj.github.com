Daily safety check

  1. Check Environment Canada weather and AQHI.
  2. Check Parks Canada bulletins/trail conditions and Alberta 511 road conditions.
  3. Pick one outdoor objective and one low-exertion fallback.
  4. In heavy smoke, skip hikes and long viewpoints; favor driving, indoor time, meals, and rest.
  5. Never enter closed areas or route around a closure.
